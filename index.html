Bio

Dury Dava stand as one of the most distinctive and compelling ensembles to emerge from the contemporary European avant-garde. Based in Athens yet resonating far beyond geographical or stylistic borders, the band crafts an arresting synthesis of psychedelic exploration, Mediterranean tonalities, and art-rock sophistication. Their music spans across several genres, paying tribute to the raw grit of 60's psychedelia and 70's krautrock, fusing elements from the Greco-Turkish musical traditions such as odd rhythms and folk dances with a punk mentality. They are known for their explosive live shows in unexpected urban venues or mystical summer festivals on remote Greek islands.

On stage, the band is raw, loud, and artfully unpolished. Shows feel less like concerts and more like controlled chaos—moments where groove and dissonance collide, stretch, and detonate. It's the kind of performance that turns a room into a collective trance. And after multiple successful month-long tours, audiences across Europe have taken notice, whilst sharing stages with kindred sonic explorers such as Beak>, Minami Deutsch, La Femme and Motorpsycho.

Dury Dava's third album, ΔΕΠΥ (translates to ADHD), was released in November 2025 via Patras-based independent label Inner Ear Records, following Dury Dava's much praised debut self-titled album released in May 2019, and Deluxe, released in April 2022.

The band intends to spend a good part of 2026 touring their new album, as well as working intensively on new material to be released in the near future.
